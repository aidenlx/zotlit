An update refreshes everything from **Source** down, and the Zotero properties. It keeps the five headings on top and what you write under them, the `status` you set, and tags you add in Obsidian. A tag you remove in Zotero stays in the note until you delete it there. A Zotero tag with a comma or a `#` needs a manual fix in Obsidian. Keep the hidden marker lines `%%zt-managed%%` and `%%/zt-managed%%`. ZotLit writes the title heading once; only the `title` property follows a title change.

To change a color's meaning or the order of the groups, follow the **Color meanings for critical reading** entry. Every note with this look follows on its next update.
