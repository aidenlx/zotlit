An update refreshes everything from **Source** down, and the Zotero properties. It keeps your answers under the five top headings, and the values you set in `status`, `date-read`, and `contribution`. Tags you add in Obsidian stay. A tag you remove in Zotero stays in the note until you delete it there. A Zotero tag with a comma or a `#` needs a manual fix in Obsidian. Keep the hidden marker lines `%%zt-managed%%` and `%%/zt-managed%%`. ZotLit writes the title heading once; only the `title` property follows a title change.

For a date picker on `date-read`, select the icon beside it once, then **Property type** > **Date**.

To change a color's meaning, follow the **Color meanings for a literature review** entry.
