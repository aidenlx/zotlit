**Update literature note** refreshes everything from **Source** down, and new annotations go into their groups. Your three headings above it stay. Keep the hidden marker lines `%%zt-managed%%` and `%%/zt-managed%%`.

ZotLit writes the title heading once. If the title changes in Zotero, edit the heading by hand.

To change a group's meaning, follow the **Color meanings** entry. Headings and callout titles follow on the next update. Two colors with the same meaning share one group.

Tags you add in Obsidian stay, and so does a `status` you change by hand. A tag you remove in Zotero stays in the note until you delete it there. A Zotero tag with a comma or a `#` needs a manual fix in Obsidian.
