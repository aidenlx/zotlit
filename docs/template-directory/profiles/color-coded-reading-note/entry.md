The colors need no CSS snippet. Obsidian has no yellow or pink callout, so yellow shows orange, magenta purple, and plum red. The callout title tells them apart. Any other color shows under **Other highlights**.

To change a meaning or a callout color, follow the **Color meanings** entry. Notes follow on their next update.

**Update literature note** refreshes everything above **My notes**. To change the text under a highlight, edit its comment in Zotero. Tags you add in Obsidian stay, and so does a `status` you change by hand. A tag you remove in Zotero stays in the note until you delete it there. A Zotero tag with a comma or a `#` needs a manual fix in Obsidian.
