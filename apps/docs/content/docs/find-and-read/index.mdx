---
title: "Find and read papers"
description: "Find a paper in your Zotero library from Obsidian, read the PDF with your highlights, and pull them into your notes."
---

- [Browse your library in the Library View](/docs/how-to/use-library-view) — Find papers by collection, tags, or reading state to open PDFs and cite.
- [Open an item's PDF in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/open-pdf-in-obsidian) — Open a paper's PDF directly in Obsidian's reader from notes or switcher.
- [Use the annotation view](/docs/how-to/use-annotation-view) — View your PDF highlights in the sidebar and bring them into notes.
- [How to edit PDF annotations in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations) — Create, change, or delete Zotero highlights inside Obsidian and save them back.
