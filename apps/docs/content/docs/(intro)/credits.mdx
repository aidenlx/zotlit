- [obsidian-zotero-integration](https://github.com/mgmeyers/obsidian-zotero-integration)
- [zotero-plugin-scaffold](https://github.com/zotero-plugin-dev/zotero-plugin-scaffold) (Zotero devtool hot-reload workflow)

## Contributors

Contributors whose proposals shipped in v2. In most cases the original pull request was closed and the idea was reimplemented during the v2 rewrite.

- [Ascarshen](https://github.com/Ascarshen) ([#459](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/459)): Zotero 8 support and citation-key handling stabilization (merged into v1)
- [DanielRunningen](https://github.com/DanielRunningen) ([#353](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/353), [#351](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/351)): full-date parsing and bulk create/update of literature notes
- [OliverDudgeon](https://github.com/OliverDudgeon) ([#433](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/433)): related items and relations in templates
- [theotheo](https://github.com/theotheo) ([#296](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/296)): folder routing by allowing `/` in filenames to specify directories
- [Tisen-Ray](https://github.com/Tisen-Ray) ([#466](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/466)): Zotero 9 support
- [yukiizumi3](https://github.com/yukiizumi3) ([#464](https://github.com/aidenlx/zotlit/pull/464)): custom Zotero column showing each item's Obsidian note status
