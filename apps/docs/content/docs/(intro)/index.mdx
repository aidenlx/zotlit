ZotLit brings your Zotero library into Obsidian. Browse your papers, read their PDFs with your Zotero highlights, and write literature notes and citations without leaving your vault.

<Callout type="warn" title="Coming from v1?">
  v2 is a major upgrade with breaking changes. Your existing literature notes
  and custom templates do not carry over unchanged: notes lose incremental
  updates until re-created, and custom Eta templates need rework. Read [Migrate
  from v1](/docs/how-to/migrate-from-v1) before upgrading.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info" title="Upgrading from 2.1?">
  Your 2.1 literature note templates keep working. ZotLit offers a one-pass
  conversion that folds them into profile documents. Until you convert, only the
  Default profile creates and updates notes. Read [Migrate from 2.1
  templates](/docs/how-to/migrate-from-2.1-templates) before you add a profile.
</Callout>

## Start here

Follow these two steps. ZotLit reads your library straight from the Zotero database, so you need no other add-on to browse it and read your papers.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Install ZotLit

    [Install and enable ZotLit in Obsidian.](/docs/install-zotlit)

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open your library

    [Find a paper in the Library View, read it beside the library, and write your first literature note.](/docs/tutorial/first-note)

  </Step>
</Steps>

Add the rest when a task needs it:

- [Keep Zotero and Obsidian in sync](/docs/how-to/keep-zotero-and-obsidian-in-sync) with ZotLit Companion, the Zotero add-on, so recent Zotero changes reach Obsidian and Zotero's menus can open your notes.
- [Install Better BibTeX](/docs/how-to/insert-citations#citation-keys) for stable citation keys when you start citing.
