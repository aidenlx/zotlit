Choose ZotLit to read and annotate PDFs, keep your highlights next to your own notes, cite, and export a Word file, all inside Obsidian, without Better BibTeX. It needs a recent Obsidian (1.14.5 or later) and Zotero 9 or 10. Choose Citations if you want only citations and simple literature notes from an exported bibliography file, including from a reference manager other than Zotero. Choose Zotero Integration if your notes already depend on its templates and it still works with your Zotero and Better BibTeX.

## Where Zotero Integration is the right choice

Choose Zotero Integration when your literature notes already depend on its Nunjucks templates. Moving those notes to ZotLit requires rewriting templates in Liquid.

Its templates also offer specific control over note imports. They can keep several separate parts of a single note on each import through multiple `persist` blocks, test whether an import is the first one with `isFirstImport`, or select only the annotations added since the previous import using `lastImportDate`. ZotLit instead uses one managed region per note and rewrites that region in full on each update. It runs on Obsidian 1.1.1 or later.

Regarding upkeep, its repository is held by the Obsidian Community Archive. The Archive's own page states that it is "a landing space for popular community plugins and themes which can no longer be taken care of by their creators", that new maintainers are sought, and that after two years of inactivity a project is archived completely. Its latest release is version 3.2.1 from 11 August 2024.

## Where Citations is the right choice

Choose Citations if you use a reference manager other than Zotero, or if you prefer an exported bibliography file over a live connection. Citations reads an exported BibLaTeX (.bib) or CSL-JSON file and operates without any external program running.

It fits workflows that only need two basic actions: creating a literature note for a paper and inserting a Markdown citation into your draft. Its templates use simple `{{variable}}` fields in Handlebars. It runs on Obsidian 0.9.20 or later. Its latest release is version 0.4.5 from 24 September 2022.

## Where ZotLit is the right choice

Choose ZotLit if you want your highlights and comments to flow directly into a literature note and stay updated, without disturbing your own thoughts written outside the managed region. It lets you open a paper and add highlights in an Obsidian tab right next to your draft. When using Zotero 10, those highlights save back to Zotero and sync across your devices.

It also supports drafting through to completion. You can insert citations by typing `[@` and export your finished chapter directly to a Word file with formatted citations and a reference list, without installing Better BibTeX.

ZotLit has specific requirements. It requires Obsidian 1.14.5 or later and Zotero 9 or 10, and it works with Zotero only. Exporting to Word requires Zotero to be running and needs the Pandoc engine installed from ZotLit's settings. Like the other two plugins, it does not run on a phone or tablet.

## Questions [#questions]

### Do I need Better BibTeX for ZotLit?

No. ZotLit reads citation keys from Zotero's own Citation Key field. Better BibTeX is optional; if you have it, the keys it makes are in that field, so ZotLit sees them.

### Can I keep my Zotero Integration notes if I switch?

Yes. They are ordinary Markdown files and stay in your vault. ZotLit leaves its begin notes markers in place as inert comments, and a note that carries a zotero-key property is recognised as a ZotLit literature note.

### Which plugin works on iPad or Android?

None of the three. All three plugins are marked desktop only in their own manifest files.

### Which plugin should I use if I do not use Zotero?

Citations. It reads a BibLaTeX or CSL-JSON file, and its README asks users of other reference managers to check whether theirs exports one. ZotLit and Zotero Integration work with Zotero only.

### Can I run ZotLit and Zotero Integration side by side?

They have different plugin IDs and can both be installed for testing. ZotLit's migration guide advises turning Zotero Integration off when you switch, because running both at once can cause conflicts with Zotero connections.
