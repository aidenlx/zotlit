## Which Zotero plugin for Obsidian is best for you? [#which-plugin]

Choose ZotLit to read and annotate PDFs, keep your highlights next to your own notes, cite, and export a Word file, all inside Obsidian. It needs a recent Obsidian (1.14.5 or later) and Zotero 9 or 10. Choose Citations if you want only citations and simple literature notes from an exported bibliography file, including from a reference manager other than Zotero. Choose Zotero Integration if your notes already depend on its templates and it still works with your Zotero and Better BibTeX.

## Where Zotero Integration fits

- Your notes are built on its Nunjucks templates; moving to ZotLit means rewriting them in Liquid.
- Its templates can keep several separate parts of a note on each import (several `persist` blocks) and pick annotations added since the last import (`lastImportDate`). ZotLit has one managed region per note and rewrites it in full on each update.
- Its repository is held by the Obsidian Community Archive, which calls itself "a landing space for popular community plugins and themes which can no longer be taken care of by their creators".

## Where Citations fits

- You use a reference manager other than Zotero, or prefer a bibliography file to a live connection: Citations reads a BibLaTeX (.bib) or CSL-JSON export and needs nothing running.
- You want only a literature note per paper and a Markdown citation.

## Where ZotLit is the weaker choice

- It needs Obsidian 1.14.5 or later; Zotero Integration accepts 1.1.1 and Citations 0.9.20.
- It reads Zotero 9 or 10 only, and Zotero only.
- Word export needs the Pandoc engine (a one-time install from ZotLit's settings) and Zotero running.

## Questions [#questions]

### Do I need Better BibTeX for ZotLit?

Not to browse your library, read PDFs or write literature notes. To cite as you write, yes: ZotLit cites a paper by the key in Zotero's own Citation Key field, and Better BibTeX is the Zotero add-on that gives every paper that key.

### Can I keep my Zotero Integration notes if I switch?

Yes. They are ordinary Markdown files and stay in your vault. ZotLit leaves its `%% begin notes %%` markers in place as inert comments, and a note that carries a zotero-key property is recognised as a ZotLit literature note.

### Which plugin works on iPad or Android?

None of the three. All three plugins are marked desktop only in their own manifest files.

### Which plugin should I use if I do not use Zotero?

Citations. It reads a BibLaTeX or CSL-JSON file, and its README asks users of other reference managers to check whether theirs exports one. ZotLit and Zotero Integration work with Zotero only.
