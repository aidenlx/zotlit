The annotation view is a sidebar panel that lists a Zotero item's annotations. You can browse highlights, drag them into your notes, and choose whether the view follows your active tab, the Zotero reader, or one item you pin.

## Open the annotation view

Open the command palette and run:

<Command name={m.command_open_annot_view_name()} />

The <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_name()} /> panel opens in the right sidebar. A ribbon icon (highlighter) also opens the view.

By default, the view follows your active tab. When that tab is neither a literature note nor a Zotero PDF, it shows <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_empty_active_tab()} />

To open a Zotero PDF in an Obsidian tab, see [Open an item's PDF in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/open-pdf-in-obsidian).

## Choose what the view follows

The annotation view has three follow modes:

| Mode                                                        | Behavior                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_active_tab()} /> (default) | Follows your active tab: the item of a literature note, or the attachment of a Zotero PDF. |
| <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_zotero_reader()} />        | Follows the attachment open in Zotero's reader. Needs live updates.                        |
| <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_pinned()} />               | Stays on one item you chose, with its attachment remembered.                               |

Select the mode button at the start of the view's toolbar to switch. The same
three switches are in the view's pane menu, and each has a command of its own.

The view keeps the mode you chose until you choose another one. When its source
cannot answer, the view says so in place instead of switching for you.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Follow the Zotero reader

    Select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_zotero_reader()} /> from the mode button. When you open or switch items in the Zotero reader, the view updates to show that attachment's annotations.

    This mode needs live updates, which carry the reader's position from Zotero to Obsidian. While live updates is off, the view offers <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_enable_live_updates()} /> in place of the list. See [Set up live updates](/docs/how-to/set-up-live-updates).

    Closing the reader in Zotero leaves the last attachment on screen under <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_reader_closed()} />

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pin one item

    Select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_pin_current_item()} /> to pin the item the view already shows, or <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_pin_choose_item()} /> to search Zotero for another one.

    Pinning from a Zotero PDF tab releases that tab's hold on the attachment, so you can switch between the item's attachments while the pin stands.

    Select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_unpin()} /> to return to the mode you were in before the pin.

    A PDF that Zotero stores with no parent item cannot be pinned, and the control says so.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose an attachment

When the item has more than one attachment, a picker under the toolbar names the one on screen with its annotation count, and remembers your choice per item.

A Zotero PDF tab and the Zotero reader each choose the attachment themselves, so the picker stands down while one of them does.

To read the attachment on screen in Obsidian, select the mode button, then select <UiLabel name={m.command_open_pdf_name()} />. The same item is in the view's pane menu. It is not shown when an Obsidian PDF tab already shows that attachment.

## Filter the list [#filter]

The toolbar has two filters. Each opens as a small popover under its button.

- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_filter_tag_trigger()} />: lists the tags on the
  annotations. Type in the search field to find a tag, then select one or more
  tags.
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_filter_color_trigger()} />: lists the colors of
  the annotations. Select one or more colors.

The list shows the annotations that have any of the selected tags and any of the selected colors. After you select a tag or a color, the button shows your choice. To clear it, open the popover and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_filter_tag_clear()} /> or <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_filter_color_clear()} />.

## Select several cards [#select-several]

Select a card to select it alone. To select more than one card:

| Action                                                      | Result                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Ctrl`-click (Windows and Linux) or `Command`-click (macOS) | Adds a card to the selection, or removes it.                   |
| `Shift`-click                                               | Selects all cards from the last card you selected to this one. |
| `Ctrl+A` (Windows and Linux) or `Command+A` (macOS)         | Selects all cards that the list shows.                         |

You can also press `Shift+Up` or `Shift+Down` to extend the selection one card at a time. Press `Escape` to clear the selection.

With the cards selected, you can:

- Delete them: press `Delete` or `Backspace`, then confirm. You can also right-click a selected card and select the delete item, which shows the number of cards.
- Change their color: press a key from `1` to `8`. The keys follow the order of Zotero's color palette. You can also right-click a selected card and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_card_color()} />.
- Copy their text: press `Ctrl+C` (Windows and Linux) or `Command+C` (macOS), or right-click a selected card and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_copy_text()} />. ZotLit copies the quoted text of each card, or its comment when it has no quoted text, with a blank line between cards.

Deleting and changing colors are edits, so editing must be enabled. One undo reverses the change on all the cards. See [How to edit PDF annotations in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations) for undo and for the other edits.

## Locked annotations [#locked]

A lock icon beside a card's page number marks an annotation that ZotLit cannot change. Point to the icon to see the reason. For example, Zotero imported the annotation from the PDF file, or another member of a group library created it. See [How to edit PDF annotations in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations) for what you can still do with a locked annotation.

## Pull annotations into a note

1. Open the note where you want to insert the annotation.
2. Drag an annotation row from the sidebar to the required editor position.
3. Wait for the insertion to complete. Image and ink excerpts are prepared before their links are inserted.

To insert without dragging, put the cursor in the note where you want the annotation. Then right-click the annotation card, or open its <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_more_tooltip()} /> menu, and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_insert()} />. ZotLit inserts the same text that a drag inserts. The item is not available when no note editor is active.

ZotLit uses the [profile](/docs/concepts/profiles) of the annotation item's literature note, or the Default profile when that item has no note. Edits made while you wait move the insertion position with the text. Press Escape to cancel a pending insertion. After insertion, one Undo removes the inserted content.

With <UiLabel name={m.settings_attachment_import_name()} /> enabled, inserted excerpts are saved as image files in the vault. With it disabled, new excerpts use an unavailable-image explanation and source link. Viewing cards or selecting a target note creates no saved image files.

You can view and insert [excerpt images](/docs/concepts/excerpt-images) with both PDF readers closed. A failed image request produces fallback text or a preserved image, and the operation reports uncertain or unavailable results in one summary.

### Context menu actions

Right-click an annotation row, or open its <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_more_tooltip()} /> menu, for additional options:

- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_edit_text()} />: opens an editor for the
  quoted text of a highlight or underline. See [Edit the quoted
  text](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations#edit-the-quoted-text).
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_copy_backlink()} />: copies a link back to
  the annotation's location in the PDF.
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_copy_text()} />: copies the quoted text, or
  the comment when the annotation has no quoted text.
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_copy_citation()} />: copies a formatted
  citation for the annotation's parent item.
- <UiLabel name={m.indexed_key_menu_copy_annotation()} />: copies the
  annotation's Zotero key.
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_insert()} />: inserts the annotation at the
  cursor in the active note, as a drag does.
- <UiLabel name={m.template_data_explorer_menu_explore()} />: opens the Template
  Data Explorer with the annotation's data.
- <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_delete()} />: deletes the annotation from
  Zotero after you confirm. See [Delete an
  annotation](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations#delete-an-annotation).

When several cards are selected, the menu has only the items that act on all of them. See [Select several cards](#select-several).
