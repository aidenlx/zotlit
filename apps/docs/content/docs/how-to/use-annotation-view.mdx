Before you pull highlights into your notes, try the guided lesson [Annotate Zotero PDFs in Obsidian](/learn/annotate-zotero-pdfs-in-obsidian) (about 10 minutes).

1. Run <Command inline name={m.command_open_annot_view_name()} /> from the command palette, or select the highlighter icon in the ribbon. The <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_name()} /> panel opens in the right sidebar.
2. Open the paper's literature note or its PDF. The panel lists that paper's highlights.
3. To write in another note, such as a chapter draft, first pin the paper: open the menu at the top of the panel and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_pin_current_item()} />.
4. Drag a highlight from the panel into your note.

## Pull highlights into a note [#pull-into-note]

1. Open the command palette and run <Command inline name={m.command_open_annot_view_name()} />, or select the highlighter icon in the ribbon. The <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_name()} /> panel opens in the right sidebar.
2. Open the paper's literature note, or open its PDF in an Obsidian tab. The panel shows a card for each of the paper's annotations. The panel follows the tab you are in (<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_active_tab()} /> mode).
3. If the note you write in is not that paper's literature note, open the menu at the top of the panel and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_pin_current_item()} />. The panel stays on this paper when you switch to your draft.
4. Open the note, then drag a card from the panel to the place in the note where the highlight should go. The highlight appears at that place. An image or ink annotation takes a moment while ZotLit prepares its image, then inserts the link. One Undo removes the whole insert.

To insert without dragging: put the cursor in the note, right-click the card (or open its <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_more_tooltip()} /> menu) and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_insert()} />.

With cards selected:

- Copy their text: press `Ctrl+C` / `Command+C`, or right-click and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_copy_text()} />. Cards are separated by a blank line.
- Change their color: press a key from `1` to `8` (Zotero's palette order), or right-click and select <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_card_color()} />.
- Delete them: press `Delete` or `Backspace` and confirm, or right-click and select the delete item (for example, <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_delete_group({ count: 3 })} />).
- Changing color and deleting change Zotero, so editing must be on; one undo reverses the change on all the cards. See [How to edit PDF annotations in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations).

## If you see something else

- **"<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_empty_active_tab()} />":** The tab you are in is neither. Open the paper's literature note or PDF, or pin the paper. To open a Zotero PDF in Obsidian, see [Open an item's PDF in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/open-pdf-in-obsidian).
- **A button "<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_enable_live_updates()} />" instead of the list:** <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_zotero_reader()} /> mode needs live updates. Select the button, or see [Set up live updates](/docs/how-to/set-up-live-updates).
- **"<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_reader_closed()} />":** You closed the reader in Zotero; the panel keeps the last PDF. Open a paper in Zotero's reader again, or switch the mode back to <UiLabel name={m.annot_view_mode_active_tab()} />.
- **The panel shows another PDF of the same paper:** A paper with several attachments has an attachment picker under the panel's header. Choose another attachment from the picker; ZotLit remembers it for that paper. To read it, open the header menu and select <UiLabel name={m.command_open_pdf_name()} />.
- **A lock icon beside a card's page number:** ZotLit cannot change that annotation. Point to the icon for the reason, such as external import from the PDF or creation by another group member. See [How to edit PDF annotations in Obsidian](/docs/how-to/edit-pdf-annotations).
- **<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_menu_insert()} /> is missing from the card menu:** No note editor is active. Click into the note, then open the menu again.
- **A card reads "<UiLabel name={m.annot_view_image_unavailable()} />":** ZotLit tried its image cache, the PDF, and Zotero's saved image, and none gave an image. The card's text and the other cards still work. Viewing cards never saves an image file in your vault.
- **The inserted note shows "<UiLabel name={m.excerpt_image_import_disabled()} />":** <UiLabel name={m.settings_attachment_import_name()} /> is off in <SettingsPath page={m.settings_page_attachments()} />. Turn it on, then insert the image highlight again. The panel itself shows images either way.
- **A notice "Excerpt images: … from Zotero with uncertain freshness, … with unchecked PDF freshness, … unavailable, … kept from the note and not refreshed.":** The insert finished; the notice names images ZotLit could not check against the PDF, or could not create. Details: [Import outcomes](/docs/reference/excerpt-images#import-outcomes).
- **An image in the panel still shows the old PDF after you replaced the file:** ZotLit reuses a cached image when the PDF keeps the same file size and modification time. Clear the cache in <SettingsPath page={m.settings_page_advanced()} /> → <UiLabel name={m.settings_excerpt_cache_clear_name()} /> → <UiLabel name={m.settings_excerpt_cache_clear_action()} />. Details: [Cache and freshness](/docs/reference/excerpt-images#freshness).
