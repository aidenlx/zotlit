Academic research is often split across two screens: Zotero holds your papers and PDFs, while Obsidian holds your thinking and drafts. You read in one app, switch to the other to write, and constantly jump back and forth just to verify a quote or check an abstract. Meanwhile, your vault fills with empty literature notes whose only job is to track a "to-read" status.

For [**Obsidian October 2026**](https://obsidian.md/blog/2026-obsidian-october/), **ZotLit 2.2** turns Obsidian into a true reading desk for your Zotero library. You can browse your collections, read and annotate PDFs side by side with your drafts, cite passages directly, and track your reading pipeline in dynamic tables. Your vault stays clean, and you write a note only when you have something to say.

Requires Obsidian 1.14.5+ desktop and the ZotLit Companion add-on for Zotero.

## Browse your Zotero library in Obsidian

When you write, the paper you need is one tab away. The **Library View** shows your Zotero libraries, collections, and tags in an Obsidian tab. Select a paper to see its abstract, its PDF, and how many annotations you made in each color. Drag a paper into a note to cite it.

![The Library View with My Library filtered to papers with PDFs, and the Reading Card of one paper showing its tags, its PDF, and its annotation counts by color](/img/blog/v2.2-library-view.webp)

When you meet a citation in a note, its popover has a **Show in Zotero library** button that takes you to the paper in the Library View.

## Read and annotate Zotero PDFs in Obsidian

Read a paper in the tab beside your draft. ZotLit opens a paper's PDF from your Zotero storage in an Obsidian tab, so your vault holds no copies. With Zotero 10, the highlights, notes, and drawings you make in Obsidian save to Zotero, and the ones you make in Zotero appear in Obsidian.

![A paper open in Obsidian's PDF reader with its Zotero highlights and underlines, one highlight's popup showing its comment and tag, and the annotation view listing each annotation as a card](/img/blog/v2.2-pdf-reader-annotations.webp)

The annotation view beside the PDF has a cleaner design: each card shows the quote, your comment, and its tags. Drag a card into your note, and ZotLit inserts the quote with a link back to the exact passage. Hover the link later to see the annotation again, above the paper's reference.

An annotation link can also be your citation. Turn on **Annotation citations** and **Show annotation links as citations** under **Settings > Citations**, and a link with no text, such as `[](zotero://…)`, shows as the paper's citation with the page, numbered with your other citations.

![A thesis chapter where an annotation link shows as the citation "(Ioannidis 2005, 696)" next to an ordinary citation of Rougier et al. 2014, with its popover open: the page-696 quote and the comment "Both apply to the pilot cohort (n = 24)" above the paper's reference buttons and bibliography entry](/img/blog/v2.2-annotation-link-as-citation.webp)

Showing annotation links as citations is opt-in and off by default. With it off, an annotation link stays a link, and its hover shows the same popover.

## Track your reading list in a table

This is where those empty notes go away. ZotLit Query shows your papers in a table, much like Obsidian Bases. Add columns such as reading status or rating, and edit them in place. A paper needs no literature note until you edit one of its cells; then ZotLit creates the note for you.

![A table of papers with reading status, note, related, done, rating, and finished columns from each literature note, with the status cell of one paper open for editing and suggesting read, reading, to-read, and unread](/img/blog/v2.2-reading-status-table.webp)

The same tables work inside a note as query blocks. The default literature note now shows the paper's annotations in a query block, which stays current as you highlight in Zotero or Obsidian.

![A literature note with an embedded query block that groups the paper's annotations by color, with the Yellow group's four cards in two columns above the Red group](/img/blog/v2.2-query-block-in-note.webp)

## Also in 2.2

- Citations appear as links in Obsidian's graph view. A paper you cite without a literature note shows as a dimmed node, so you can see which papers your notes have in common.
- A literature note profile is now one document, and the Template Workbench edits it beside a live preview of the note.
- Search and the citation picker stay responsive in large libraries, and an optional download finds more Chinese titles in search.
- The ZotLit Query CLI lets your scripts and AI agents query your Zotero library through the Obsidian CLI.

For every change in 2.2, read the [2.2.0 changelog](/changelog/2.2.0).

## Get ZotLit 2.2

1. In Obsidian, open **Settings > Community plugins**, select **Browse**, and install or update **ZotLit**.
2. In Zotero, install the matching [ZotLit Companion](/docs/install-companion) add-on.
3. Open the command palette and run **ZotLit: Open Library View** or **ZotLit: New Query File**.
