import { Check, Fix, Step } from "@/components/learn/lesson-mdx";
import { UiLabel } from "@/components/ui-label";
import { m } from "@/paraglide/messages.js";

<Step n={1}>

Open **Settings**, then **ZotLit**, then <UiLabel name={m.settings_page_citations()} />. At <UiLabel name={m.settings_citation_engine_name()} />, select <UiLabel name={m.settings_citation_engine_install()} />. This engine comes with ZotLit; you do not install Pandoc yourself. You do this once on each computer.

<Check>
  The row reads{" "}
  <UiLabel
    name={m.settings_citation_engine_status_installed({ version: "3.10" })}
  />{" "}
  (the number can be higher).
</Check>

</Step>

<Step n={2}>

In `Chapter 3 - Presenting the results`, place your cursor where the citation goes and type `[@sandve`.

<Check>
  A list opens under the cursor. The row shows the title, then **Sandve et al.
  (2013), PLOS Computational Biology, 9(10), e1003285**.
</Check>

<Fix title="The paper is not in the list">
  In Zotero, look at the paper's **Citation Key** field. A paper with no key
  cannot be cited. Better BibTeX, a Zotero add-on, fills this field for every
  paper.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={3}>

Press Enter to choose the highlighted paper from the list.

<Check>The draft holds `[@sandve2013]`.</Check>

</Step>

<Step n={4}>

Before the closing bracket, type `, p. 2`, so it reads `[@sandve2013, p. 2]`. Move the cursor away.

<Check>The citation shows as **(Sandve et al. 2013, 2)**.</Check>

<Fix title="It still reads [@sandve2013, p. 2]">
  The engine is not installed. Do step 1.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={5}>

Point your cursor at the formatted citation.

<Check>
  A preview shows the full reference: Sandve, Geir Kjetil, and others, 2013,
  "Ten Simple Rules for Reproducible Computational Research", PLOS Computational
  Biology 9 (10): e1003285.
</Check>

</Step>

<Step n={6}>

Open the paper's PDF beside the draft. In the sidebar on the right, drag the page label of a highlight, such as **p. 2**, into the draft.

<Check>
  The draft holds a quote block titled **Page 2**. Under the title are the
  highlighted passage with its citation and page, and the comment from Lesson 2.
  The title links back to the passage.
</Check>

</Step>
