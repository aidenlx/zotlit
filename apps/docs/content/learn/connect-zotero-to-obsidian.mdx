import { Check, Figure, Fix, Step } from "@/components/learn/lesson-mdx";
import { UiLabel } from "@/components/ui-label";
import { m } from "@/paraglide/messages.js";

<Step n={1}>

In Obsidian, open **Settings**, select **Community plugins**, and choose **Browse**. Search for `ZotLit`. Select **Install**, then select **Enable**. If Obsidian asks before browsing, select **Turn on community plugins**.

<Check>
  A tab named <UiLabel name={m.library_view_name()} /> opens. At the top sits a
  badge reading <UiLabel name={m.welcome_badge()} /> with four setup steps above
  your list of papers.
</Check>

<Fix title="A notice says Obsidian's installer is too old">
  Download Obsidian again from obsidian.md/download, install it over your
  existing version, and enable ZotLit.
</Fix>

<Fix title="No tab opened">
  Select the ZotLit icon in the ribbon on the left edge of Obsidian.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={2}>

Look at the first setup step, <UiLabel name={m.welcome_step_connect_title()} />. Once you see a green check, close the setup section by selecting the × button at its top right. Leave the other three setup items as they are.

<Check>
  <UiLabel name={m.welcome_step_connect_title()} /> shows a green check
  alongside your Zotero data folder (such as `~/Zotero`) and item count. After
  closing the setup section, your Zotero papers fill the tab.
</Check>

<Fix title={<>The tab reads <UiLabel name={m.library_view_not_found()} /></>}>Open Zotero, go to **Settings** > **Advanced**, and check the path under **Data Directory Location**. Return to Obsidian, select <UiLabel name={m.library_view_locate()} />, and choose that folder.</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={3}>

Click into <UiLabel name={m.library_view_search_placeholder()} /> and type a few words from the title, such as `reproducible`. Select the paper from the search results.

<Check>
  A card opens below the list showing the title, authors, journal, and year
  (*PLOS Computational Biology, 9(10), e1003285 · 2013*), along with two
  buttons: <UiLabel name={m.library_view_menu_open_pdf()} /> and{" "}
  <UiLabel name={m.library_view_menu_write_note()} />.
</Check>

<Fix title="The list is empty">
  Return to Step 2 and confirm that{" "}
  <UiLabel name={m.welcome_step_connect_title()} /> has a green check.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={4}>

On the paper's card, select <UiLabel name={m.library_view_menu_open_pdf()} />. ZotLit opens the PDF directly from where Zotero stores it without making a duplicate copy.

<Check>
  The PDF opens in a new tab. Your highlights appear in their original colours,
  with a sidebar on the right listing each highlight, comment, and tag.
</Check>

<Fix title="No highlights show">
  Scroll to a page containing highlights. If the paper has none in Zotero yet,
  proceed to Step 5; you will add them in Lesson 2.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={5}>

Switch back to the <UiLabel name={m.library_view_name()} /> tab. On the paper's card, select <UiLabel name={m.library_view_menu_write_note()} />.

<Check>
  A notice reads <UiLabel name={m.notice_created_note()} /> A new note named
  after the citation key (`sandve2013`) opens in the `literatures` folder. It
  displays the title, links to Zotero and the PDF, and an **Annotations**
  section listing your highlights.
</Check>

<Fix
  title={
    <>
      The button reads <UiLabel name={m.library_view_menu_open_note()} />
    </>
  }
>
  A literature note already exists for this paper. Select the button to open it.
</Fix>

</Step>

<Step n={6}>

Click directly beneath the line containing the two links. Type a heading, such as `My thoughts`, and write how this paper supports your thesis chapter.

<Check>Your text appears above the **Annotations** section.</Check>

<Figure>

| Note area                          | What belongs here                                     | What ZotLit does                                           |
| :--------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your text** (e.g. `My thoughts`) | Your summary, synthesis, and ideas for your draft     | Never touches or overwrites it; never sends it to Zotero   |
| **Annotations**                    | Highlights, tags, and comments shown live from Zotero | Manages this section automatically and keeps it up to date |

</Figure>

<Fix title="There are no highlight cards">
  The paper has no highlights in Zotero yet. They will appear here automatically
  once you add one.
</Fix>

</Step>
