## 3.1 Why the figures come first

A reader decides in seconds whether a figure is worth their time, so each figure in this chapter has one message, stated before it is drawn [@rougier2014, p. 1]. The same data can support very different stories, and the chart type follows from the story rather than from habit.

## 3.2 How much to trust a single result

Before comparing the three cohorts, I set out how much weight a single significant result can bear. Small samples, small effects, and flexible analyses all lower the chance that a finding is true [@ioannidis2005]. Every comparison below reports its effect size and its confidence interval, not only the p-value.
