## Files and changes

- `sakimas-song.pdf` is the unmodified Storybooks Canada imageless PDF. SHA-256:
  `c16a4daca0352fad9fec09a59083ed2b2e36cd8e963395a8dd79ebb4432437e5`.
- `sakimas-song.html` is an adaptation of the English Storybooks Canada story
  page. It keeps the title, English story text, writer, illustrator, source,
  and license attribution. It removes images, audio, navigation, translations,
  scripts, and other remote resources. It adds small inline styles for an
  offline reading layout. SHA-256:
  `41dd001c025c2e3fce1464583154bf9eec72534242bed778436d34be99705a44`.

Annotation data is separate from the PDF. The Fixture generator writes the
Annotation rows to `zotero.sqlite` and does not modify the PDF.
